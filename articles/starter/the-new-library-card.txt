"Is this your first visit?" the woman at the desk asked.

"Yes," Ben said. "I moved here last week. I would like a library card."

The woman gave him a short form. He wrote his name and address, then showed her a letter with his new address on it. A few minutes later, she handed him a blue card.

"You can borrow three books today," she said. "They are due back in two weeks."

Ben chose a small book about plants and a story with short chapters. He left the third space free. There was no need to choose everything at once.

On the way home, he put the card in his wallet. The town felt a little more familiar now.
