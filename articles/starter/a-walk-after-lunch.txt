After lunch, Maya closed her computer and stepped outside. She had only ten minutes before her next meeting, so she chose the small park across the street.

An old man was sitting on a bench. A brown dog was sleeping beside his feet. Near the gate, two children were trying to catch a ball. Maya walked slowly past them and followed the path around the pond.

She did not check her messages. Instead, she listened to the birds and watched the light on the water. On her way back, she bought a piece of fruit for later.

The afternoon was still full of work, but she felt more awake. A short walk had given her a little space to breathe.
