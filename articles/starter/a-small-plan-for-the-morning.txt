I used to start each morning by looking at my phone. Before I noticed the time, I was already late. One Sunday, I decided to try a smaller and calmer plan.

Now I put a glass of water beside the bed. When I wake up, I drink it and open the curtains. Then I write down one thing I want to finish that day. It can be a simple job, such as washing my clothes or calling a friend.

I still have busy mornings. Sometimes I miss the bus, and sometimes I forget my lunch. The plan does not fix everything. It just helps me begin with one clear thought. That is enough for today.
